# Hogar360 Insights Research Notes

## Evidence to Use Carefully

| Source | Supported lesson for Hogar360 | Editorial boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harvard Business School, analysis of more than 75,000 Los Angeles-area sales | Virtual tours can help buyers screen properties and avoid unnecessary visits, but should not be presented as a universal guarantee of higher sale prices or faster sales. | Explain that a tour supports a more informed early conversation, rather than promising a selling outcome. |
| National Association of REALTORS®, *Your Virtual Tour Playbook Is Here* (2025) | Virtual tours give buyers more context on layout and can help them judge suitability before scheduling an in-person visit. | Frame the service as presentation and qualification support for agents, not a replacement for client service or in-person visits. |
| Zillow 3D Home | In Zillow’s own measurements, listings with interactive floor plans received more views, saves, and shares than comparable listings without them in the specified U.S. sample. | Identify the figures as Zillow platform data from U.S. markets; do not project the numbers to Santiago or the Dominican Republic. |
| *The Impact of Virtual Marketing Strategies on the Price–TOM Relation* (Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics, 2022) | Effects differ by property context, price segment, and occupancy. Virtual tours can be especially relevant for homes that are harder to show. | Use this to emphasize strategy and fit rather than universal claims.

## Starter Article Angles

1. **La visita empieza antes de la visita.** Explain how a 3D tour helps a prospect understand layout, rule out poor fits, and arrive with better questions.
2. **Qué está cambiando en otros mercados.** Describe how major real-estate platforms use virtual tours and interactive plans, carefully distinguishing platform data from local promises.
3. **Cómo preparar una propiedad para un recorrido 3D.** A practical checklist on access, light, organization, preparation, and choosing the contact path.
