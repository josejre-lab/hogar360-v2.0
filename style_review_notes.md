# Hogar360 Visual Review Notes

The minimalist redesign renders cleanly on both routes. The warm off-white canvas, deep navy anchors, restrained teal accents, oversized Spanish headlines, spacious editorial rhythm, and practical copy voice are working well.

The accepted refinements are to make Hogar360 feel more ownable than a generic real-estate/SaaS template. The logo should move beyond a plain initial and use a 360/property-view identity cue. A recurring spatial motif should appear across the pages through section markers, viewport framing, scan/coordinate details, or orbit cues. Portfolio imagery should remain real but feel more curated through consistent crops and editorial labels. Teal should remain a selective signature accent.

The generated professional architectural background is now used behind both hero sections with a light text-safe overlay. The Matterport area is intentionally a preparation state until the final link exists.
